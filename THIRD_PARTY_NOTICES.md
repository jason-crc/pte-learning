## AWL Focus headword dataset

- Project: AWL Focus (`Jianyuanxi/AWL`)
- Repository: https://github.com/Jianyuanxi/AWL
- Pinned source commit: `168c3614f053a67ef0ec5cd115db34c5dc54a345`
- Source file: `assets/words.json`
- Repository license: MIT License; copied in `data/AWL_LICENSE`
- Use: 570 English headwords and their Academic Word List sublists

The underlying Academic Word List was developed by Averil Coxhead. Academic source: Coxhead,
A. (2000), *A New Academic Word List*, TESOL Quarterly, 34(2), 213–238.

This repository uses the AWL as a third-party PTE Academic preparation set. It is not an official
Pearson word list and does not imply Pearson recommendation or endorsement.
